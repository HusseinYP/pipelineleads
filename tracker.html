What it does

Stop losing track of who said yes.

Built for shops that quote jobs from a phone, a notebook and a pile of texts.

  • Quotes sent, won, lost and your win rate for any month you pick.
  • Quotes to chase: any quote older than your follow-up days with no answer is flagged, with the phone number, so you can call.
  • Money owed to you: unpaid balances, plus work completed by month.
  • Booked work: everything approved, scheduled or in progress, in one list.
  • Last 6 months at a glance, so you can see slow and busy seasons.
  • Dropdowns for job type, source and status, and a Chased checkbox. The formula columns are grayed and protected so you can't break them by accident.

Good questions

Before you buy.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in the free Google Sheets app. The first two columns stay put while you scroll, so you always see the customer and the date.

Can I add my own job types?

Yes, on the Settings tab. Keep the statuses as they are; the Dashboard counts them.

Do you see my data?

No. The copy is yours, in your own Google or Excel. Nothing is sent anywhere. With a done-for-you setup we only see what you share with us, and we remove our access at hand-over if you ask.

Will Google show a warning for the bot?

With the Setup + Auto-alert Bot packages, yes, once: a "Google hasn't verified this app" box when you click Allow. That's Google's standard notice for any small script inside your own sheet. We'll send you a picture guide, and you can remove the bot's access any time from your Google account. The $19 template has no script and no warning.

Do I need the kit if I have the tracker?

Only if you send quotes and invoices on paper or by text. The tracker keeps score of jobs and money; the kit writes the quote and the invoice and lists who is overdue. They are separate files on purpose, so nothing breaks when you move one. The bundle has both plus Lite for $39.

Refunds?

No, all sales are final, on templates and on setup packages. You get template files right away, so look through the pictures first. The free Lite tracker shows how the sheets work, but it doesn't include quotes or invoices. With a setup package, we confirm your details by email before we start, and one round of changes within 7 days is included. We only refund a billing mistake, a setup we can't deliver, or where the law requires it.

Is this the same as your lead-reply service?

No. The tracker is a one-time spreadsheet for jobs you already have. The lead-reply service answers requests from your website instantly and alerts your phone; it has a monthly fee and a free 7-day pilot. You can have one without the other.
