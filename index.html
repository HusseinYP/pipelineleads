Before we talk

Good questions.

Is the 7-day pilot really free?

Yes. No card, no automatic charge. After 7 days, unless you choose a plan, instant replies, alerts, follow-ups and review requests pause automatically and your request form asks customers to call you. We email you a reminder with the payment link 2 days before, and paying by card turns everything back on within 1 to 2 business days. Your leads stay yours either way. We just ask for a short feedback chat at the end. A testimonial is optional, and nothing is published without your written OK.

Do I need a new website or tech skills?

No new website. You click one link, press Set up, then Allow (see the 3-step picture guide). Google shows a warning screen first, because it's your own private copy, and the guide shows what to click. Then you add a “Request a plumber” button to your website, or send it to whoever runs your site. Stuck? Email us and we'll help. We never ask for your passwords.

Will this bring more visitors to my site?

No. It's not ads or SEO. It helps you answer the requests you already get, fast.

I already use Jobber or have a receptionist.

Check what you have first. If it already answers web requests fast and follows up, you may not need us. We won't sell you a duplicate.

Does my phone get a text?

Today the alert comes by email. Turn on email notifications and tap to call from the alert. Text alerts are in development.

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. Every customer who ticked the email box gets the same friendly review request after the job. We never filter or push for positive reviews.

Who owns the data, and can I cancel?

You do, and no one else is entitled to your data or your leads. They stay in your own Google account, and our system never sends them to us. And yes, cancel any time by email before your next charge. Your service runs through the month you've paid for, you aren't charged again, and months already paid aren't refunded. If you gave us access to anything, we remove it. We keep only billing records for tax and legal requirements, plus your shop's basic account details, and we never sell any of it.
